Things to do in Castel Gandolfo

A short guide written by the people who host in Castel Gandolfo. For current times and prices, always check the official websites.

Apostolic Palace and Papal Villas

Castel Gandolfo has been the Popes' summer residence for centuries. The Apostolic Palace faces the town's main square; the Papal Villas, with the gardens of Villa Barberini, can be visited on tours organised by the Vatican Museums. Check times and booking on the official site.

Lake Albano

The lake is an ancient volcanic crater, a short walk from the town. You can walk its shore, rent kayaks and canoes, and look down on all of it from the town above.

Lake Nemi

A few kilometres away, smaller and quieter. The town of Nemi has the Museum of the Roman Ships.

Castelli Romani Regional Park

Trails and woods above the lakes, good for a morning or a full day on foot.

Rome and the airport

Rome is about 25 km away: the regional train to Roma Termini (FL4 line) leaves from Castel Gandolfo station. Ciampino airport is 9 km away.

Times and tours can change.
